1. The Business Model

You are not selling AI software. You are selling an always-on employee that lives inside the client's existing platform—usually Slack or Telegram—and performs a specific job.

Core offer: "I build you an AI team member that handles [exact task] 24/7. You never manage it. I manage it for you."
Price: £5,000/month per client
Delivery: Live in their Slack/Telegram within 48 hours of signed agreement

What the client gets

  • A Hermes agent with a custom identity tuned to one job
  • Platform integration: it joins their Slack/Telegram as a real team member
  • Custom skills derived from their actual business context
  • Ongoing support and improvements handled by you
  • Weekly iterations based on how they use it

What the client does NOT manage

  • No hosting bills
  • No API key maintenance
  • No "AI training"
  • No uptime monitoring
Target real businesses. Verified guidance: target SMBs doing $1M–$2M+ in revenue. They pay faster, ask fewer questions, and value outcomes over novelty.

3. Role Split

The system works because the human and agent each do what they are best at. Do not blur these lines.

You — humanYour agent — build/ops
Discovery callsBuild the Hermes agent profile and SOUL.md
Understand the client's painDeploy as background gateway process
Close the dealConfigure platform tokens: Slack bot or Telegram bot
Introduce the agent to the clientAdd custom skills based on client context
Answer business relationship questionsMonitor health/restart/logs
Collect paymentSend test message to confirm live
Rule: Never tell the client "the agent builds itself." Say: "I have a build system. You get the agent by Friday." The agent is your leverage, not your sales pitch.

3.1 What to say on a discovery call — exact script

1Ask: "What task costs your team 2+ hours every single day?"
2Ask: "What happens when that task slips?"
3Ask: "If this was handled 24/7 without you managing it, what would that unlock?"
4Say: "I can build that for you as an AI team member in your Slack. £5,000/month, live within 48 hours."
5Ask: "Do you want me to send onboarding instructions today?"

3.2 Hourly value framing

If the client's time is valued at £40/hr and the task takes 2 hours daily, the task costs £2,400/month before mistakes and turnover. A £5,000 AI employee pays for itself before your first two bad hires.

4. The 80/20 Stack

Every component below is confirmed in production from Nick's public repos and posts.

4.1 Harness

1Hermes Agent — runtime and CLI. Runs on Linux, macOS, or Orgo cloud computer. Handles skills, memory, sessions, tool execution, and gateway.

4.2 Model

2gpt-5.5 via Nous (default in Nick's stack). Swappable to gpt-5.6-sol via OpenAI-Codex. Grok 4.5 for speed/low-reasoning flows.

Telegram alternative. Nick's stack supports QR onboarding without BotFather.

10. Competitors & How to Win

Every prospect is silently comparing you to five alternatives. Here is how to beat each one without sounding defensive.

10.1 Offshore freelancer / virtual assistant

Their pitch: "Your dedicated remote worker for £3–8/hr."
Their weakness: hidden management cost, inconsistency, holiday cover, language risk.
Kill shot: "You are not paying for hours. You are buying output that obeys your process every single time it runs. No sick days, no supervision, no turnover."

10.2 SaaS chatbot builder

Their pitch: "Automate support with AI chat."
Their weakness: point solution, no memory, no execution, no judgment.
Kill shot: "A chatbot answers questions. An AI employee closes loops. If your tool can't do a 7-step process without human intervention, it's software — not an employee."

10.3 Generic AI consultancy

Their pitch: "We assess, strategise, and build a proof of concept."
Their weakness: expensive, slow, often leaves nothing live.
Kill shot: "We don't do decks. We ship a live agent in your Slack within 48 hours, then improve it weekly."

10.4 In-house hire

Their pitch: "Real person on payroll, 37.5 hrs/week."
Their weakness: £25–45K/yr + NI + pension + holiday + churn.
Kill shot: "A junior costs £35K+ in year one. Our employee costs £5K/month, works every hour, and doesn't quit."

11. Contracts, SLA and Scope Protection

Use these sections to protect yourself and set expectations. Copy into your proposal.

11.1 Minimum viable terms

1Term: 30-day minimum, then month-to-month.
2Pricing: £5,000/month per single role. Additional roles: +£2,500/month each.
3Delivery: Live agent delivered within 48 hours of signed agreement and intake material.
4Refund: Partial refund if the agent misses the agreed target by day 30.

11.2 Scope-in / scope-out

In scope: one defined role, one platform, weekly skill refinements, uptime monitoring, direct chat support.
Out of scope: custom integrations outside the stack, brand campaigns, hiring the agent an assistant, changing role scope mid-month without a new statement of work.

11.3 SLA

1Uptime target: 95% during client's stated working hours.
2Response window: acknowledge within 4 business hours; fix or patch within 24 business hours.
3Data: client retains ownership of all business data; agent memory is wiped on contract end.

11.4 Proposal checklist

  • One-page scope statement: role, platform, success criteria
  • Pricing and term
  • Delivery date commitment
  • Refund or pilot clause
  • Signatures / email acceptance
  • Onboarding instructions attached
