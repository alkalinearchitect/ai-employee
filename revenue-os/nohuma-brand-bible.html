NOHUMA Brand Bible
The standard.
How every NOHUMA asset is built: palette, type, voice, and the one rule that overrides all others.
For Human Architect. Updated 2026-08-09. v2.
Palette
Near-black. Gunmetal. One violet.
Background #08080B (never pure #000)
Panel #0E0E13
Body #C3C9D2, captions #828990
Headline #FFFFFF
Accent #8b5cf6 (violet, the only colour)
No gold. No green. No gradients. No second hue.
Type
Inter. Perfect Fourth.
Font: Inter only. Never Helvetica/Arial.
Scale locked at ratio 1.333 (Perfect Fourth)
Body floor 28px. Never smaller.
Six hierarchy levels minimum per asset
Harmony comes from the ratio, not from taste.
Layout
One idea per spread.
Asymmetric left grid. Massive negative space. Full-bleed 9x12, margin zero. The brand is restraint, not decoration.
Voice
WHY first. Always.
Simon Sinek's Golden Circle: why, then how, then what. Lead with belief, not the feature. Conviction, not explanation. No em dashes. No AI tells.
The One Rule
Match the site.
Every PDF, deck, and page inherits this palette and scale. If it does not match the live site, it is wrong, regardless of who built it. The brand is one system.
