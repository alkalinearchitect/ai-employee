The 8 Components
An agent is not a prompt. It is a worker with tools.
A computer of its own (dedicated VM)
An email address in the client's name
A phone number or live messaging line
The client's channels (Slack, Telegram, iMessage)
Real tools and connectors (1,000+ apps)
A way to spend (an agent card)
A knowledge base, one fact one home
Observability across the fleet
Miss one and it is a toy. All eight and it is a worker.
Architecture
One brain. Four specialists.
Build one strong general-purpose orchestrator. It talks to the client 99% of the time and hands off to specialised sub-agents: outbound lead generation, sales follow-up, content and assets, and support.
Why isolate? 'If you can create sub-agents, you minimise the blast radius of things that can go wrong.' One failing specialist cannot sink the whole operation.
Fulfilment
Onboard it like a new employee.
Crawl, walk, run. Set it up in Slack. Let the client talk to it. Do not oneshot a custom build. The first week looks like training an intern on day three.
Most clients live in Telegram and Slack. The agent self-configures those channels. A client is live in 48 hours.
The Offer
Charge up front. Deliver in under 7 days. Or refund.
Old model: a 30 to 45 day custom build on a weak retainer. New model: take the retainer up front, ship one full automation in under a week, and guarantee it. If not fulfilled in 7 days, the money comes back.
'You give them an entire automation under 7 days. You just infiltrated their business with the agent.' Then expand to a fleet, and help them resell it to their own customers.
