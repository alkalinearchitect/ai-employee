The truth about the sector
The labour is gone. The admin stayed.
UK construction needs 206,000 more workers by 2030 (CITB, 2026 — about 41,200 a year). Wages rose 6.9% in the year to Nov 2024 and the National Living Wage hit £12.21 in April 2025. Yet the work that does not build — tendering, compliance, chasing, reporting — has not shrunk one inch.
You cannot hire your way out of a 206,000-person gap by 2030. You can automate the non-building work so the humans you do have stay on site, billable, and off the screen.
That is the wedge. Not "replace builders." Remove the administrative tax on every contract.
The bottleneck map
Five ways construction leaks money
01Tendering & PQQ admin. SMEs spend significant time on PQQs, method statements and financial packs with no guarantee of winning. Larger firms bury them under specialist bid teams.
02Business development. No one is consistently reaching the right buyer — facilities managers, estates teams, procurement leads — because the founder is on site.
03Payment & cashflow. Late payment is the sector's most overlooked crisis. Chasing takes hours and gets deprioritised.
04Compliance burden. Building Safety Act, Homes Act / Awaab's Law, PAS 2035, H&S — documentation that grows while site teams shrink.
05Reporting & coordination. Daily logs, snag lists, handovers, subcontractor and supply-chain comms eat the day.
Every segment below suffers at least three of these. NHUMA is built to remove them.
