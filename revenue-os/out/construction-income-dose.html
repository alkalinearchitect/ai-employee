POI finder method
Get the name in 10 minutes
LinkedIn: search "[Company] + [Role]" (e.g. "Mitie + Business Development Director"). Free tier shows names + tenure.
Companies House: officers list for Ltd/PLC — real directors, free.
Firm website: "Leadership" / "Our people" page — often has photos + emails.
Trade press: Building, Construction News, FMJ — appointment stories name movers (how I confirmed 4 POIs here).
Job ads: the role you want is usually being hired — the hiring manager's name is in the ad.
Never guess an email from a pattern. Pull the real one. A wrong guess burns the relationship.
