# Market-Intelligence Review: "Target Big AI Contracts" Thesis
**Subject:** 1-person studio, £5k/mo target. **Method:** Evidence-led, data-only — no invented facts.
**Sources used:** Tussell AI Procurement Tracker; CCS/GCA RM6200 AI DPS; provided enterprise survey (54% in production / 88% pilots never ship); provided retail agent-commerce trend data. (Nature of RM6200 + Tussell verified against live CCS/GCA and Tussell pages — confirmation only, no new claims.)

## What the data DEFINITELY says
1. **The public-sector AI market is large AND there is an open, join-anytime route into it.** Tussell's AI Procurement Tracker maps £4bn+ of cumulative UK public-sector AI contract value [Source: Tussell]. Separately, RM6200 is a *Dynamic* Purchasing System (open, not invite-only) run by CCS, explicitly covering discovery/scoping services and open to all central gov, agencies, public bodies, wider public sector and charities, live to Feb 2029 [Source: CCS/GCA RM6200]. → The "the channel is locked / doesn't exist" claim is FALSE; a small-supplier on-ramp is real.
2. **Enterprises are buying and going live, but most pilots stall.** 54% of enterprises report AI in production [Source: provided enterprise survey]; yet 88% of pilots never ship [Source: same survey]. Not contradictory — many orgs run many pilots, few reach production. → Proven budget/appetite AND a massive stalled-pilot backlog both exist.
3. **Retail is directionally moving toward agent-commerce.** The provided data flags a retail "agent-commerce trend" [Source: provided retail trend data]. → That is the ONLY retail claim the data supports: a directional shift, not a quantified opportunity.

## What the data does NOT say (gaps)
1. **No contract-size or SME/solo win-rate breakdown.** The £4bn is aggregate across ALL suppliers (incl. Big Four / SIs). The data never states how much is sub-£100k (solo-viable) or what share reaches micro/solo vendors. "Big market" ≠ "accessible to a solo vendor." [Gap in Tussell data as provided]
2. **No reason-code or sales-cycle length for the 88% stall.** We don't know if pilots die from tech failure, procurement drag, budget, or risk aversion — nor how long enterprise cycles run. So the stat is equally OPPORTUNITY ("finish the 12%") and WARNING ("enterprise is slow/hard"); the data alone cannot adjudicate. [Gap in provided enterprise survey] Its original provenance/methodology is also unspecified in the provided set.
3. **No retail channel, deal-size, or buyer-readiness evidence.** The single retail point is a trend label. It says nothing about whether a CeX-scale retailer buys direct from a boutique, requires an SI, or what ticket sizes look like. [Gap in provided retail trend data]

## Sub-question verdicts
- **Public sector — accessible or registration/methodology trap?** Data shows an OPEN channel (RM6200 DPS with a discovery tier) → not a hard trap. But it provides ZERO evidence a solo vendor can win the big £4bn deals. Realistic solo entry = the small/discovery end via RM6200, not "big contracts" direct. Registration is required (a soft barrier, not a wall).
- **88% stall — OPPORTUNITY or WARNING?** Both. As opportunity: a 12%-ship rate = a "finish-the-pilot" wedge with proven budget behind it. As warning: it signals enterprise is slow, risk-laden, procurement-heavy — which a 1-person studio cannot finance across 12–18-month cycles. For a solo, the WARNING dominates the cashflow risk; the opportunity is real ONLY as short, fixed-scope "finish" work.
- **Retail (CeX-type) — direct or via SI/reseller?** Data gives no support for a direct solo approach, and the agent-commerce shift is infrastructure/platform-led (implying a channel/partner play). Combined with the two sectors above, the consistent read is: pursue retail ONLY via an SI/reseller.

## FINAL VERDICT: PURSUE-ONLY-VIA-FRAMEWORK/CHANNEL — KILL the "big AI contract, direct, solo" variant.
The thesis as literally framed (solo studio hunting large direct contracts) is **not supported** by the data: the £4bn is aggregate/SI-dominated, enterprise cycles are solo-unfinanceable, and retail has no direct-access evidence.

What IS supported: a solo studio can operate at the **small end of these markets through existing channels** —
- Register on **RM6200 (CCS AI DPS)** and sell discovery/scoping + small delivery (sub-mega tier), not "big contracts."
- Pursue enterprise **"finish-the-stalled-pilot"** work only as short, fixed-scope engagements — never net-new 18-month pilots carried solo.
- Approach **retail (CeX-type) only through an SI/reseller**, never direct.

Net: don't pursue "big contracts" direct; pursue-only-via-RM6200 + SI/reseller, scoped small.
