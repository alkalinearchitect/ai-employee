# NOHUMA — 90-Day Channel Closability Assessment
**Studio reality:** 1 person · £5k/mo flat offer · live in 48h, self-hosted · NO framework registration · NO case studies · Human Architect brand.
**Brutal truth up front:** A 1-person studio at £5k/mo cannot "win a big AI contract" (£4bn public-sector call-off, CeX-scale enterprise deal) in 90 days. Those require case studies, security accreditation, bid capacity, and delivery scale you do not have. The realistic game is *borrowed credibility* + *product that demos itself* + *channel leverage*.

## The Single Highest-Probability Closable Path

**→ (E) SME-to-mid reseller partnerships.**

**Why, bluntly:**
- **Matches the unit economics.** £5k/mo per client is a natural fit for an SME/mid buyer a reseller already serves — not a £4bn framework lot you can't deliver.
- **Borrowed credibility kills the no-case-study problem.** The reseller's existing client trust substitutes for the references you don't have. Your live-in-48h, self-hosted demo does the selling; the partner supplies the name.
- **Fastest path to cash.** 30–45 days vs 6–12 months for tenders/frameworks. You ship a product; partners sell it.
- **Scales without headcount.** Each reseller is a force multiplier — 5 resellers × 2 clients = 10 × £5k = £50k/mr, all while you stay 1 person doing delivery, not sales.
- **What a 1-person studio CANNOT win:** public-sector framework call-offs, enterprise flagship "big AI contracts," anything gated by PQQ/insurance/case-study walls. Channel leverage is the only 90-day route that respects those limits.

**First 30 days for E:** one-pager + margin sheet → 20–30 targeted MSP/agency/consultant outreaches → 5 discovery calls → 2–3 signed resellers → first reseller-led client live before day 45.
