SECTION 01

The Category — why now

A managed AI employee is the 2026 equivalent of starting a Facebook-ads agency in 2013. The infrastructure just matured; the market has not caught up.

Until 2025, "AI for business" meant chatbots that answered questions and dashboards that needed a human to act. The managed-AI-employee model flips that: one agent, inside the client's workspace, owns a recurring outcome — not a conversation. The agent does the work, not just advises on it.

Proof the category is real (market evidence, not our product): Nick Vasilescu publicly reports closing £5k/month clients for design agencies, with his own Hermes agent building and onboarding the client agents. Greg Isenberg's May 2026 podcast productised the playbook: charge $5k/month for OpenClaw agents, $10k for Hermes agents, sell into legacy verticals. The model is no longer a bet — it is a pattern.

The opportunity is not "build AI." It is deploy and operate AI as a managed service for businesses that do not want to touch the stack. That is the seat Owl occupies.

  1. Retainer up front. You are paid before you build.
  2. One full automation in <7 days. (Owl does 48h.)
  3. Money-back if not fulfilled. De-risks the yes.
  4. Then expand. Fleet → resell → second revenue line.
