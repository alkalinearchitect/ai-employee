The conversation

Three questions. One defined task. One live agent.

You do not need a pitch deck. You need to diagnose the exact task that is costing them more than £5,000/month in human hours and missed opportunities.

"What task costs 2+ hours every single day?"

If they cannot name it instantly, they are not ready. If they name it without hesitating, you are already inside.

"What happens when that task slips?"

This turns a time cost into a business risk. Risk justifies £5,000/month faster than convenience ever will.

"What would you do with the hours back this week?"

This forces them to paint the outcome. If the outcome is specific, close. If it is vague, they are not ready.

"I can build that for you as an AI team member in your Slack. £5,000/month. Live within 48 hours. Do you want onboarding instructions today?"

Objections

Objections are requests for reassurance, not rejection.

Acknowledge. Reframe. Close.

ObjectionHandler
"£5,000 is too expensive"If the task takes 2 hours daily at £40/hr, it costs £2,400/month before mistakes. Owl pays for itself before your first two bad hires.
"We don't do AI here"Most clients said the same. The agent lives in Slack. You don't touch code. You just watch the work get done.
"We already have tools"Tools are not fulfillment. A dashboard is a job. Owl ships the work. What task is still manual even though you have tools?
"We tried AI before"Most AI projects fail because they ship a toy, not a worker. Owl is managed execution, not a demo. What broke last time?
"We're too small"SMBs at £1M–£2M+ pay faster and ask fewer questions. They value outcomes over novelty.
"We need custom"Every agent is custom by default — built from your knowledge, your workflow, your outputs. Can we scope exactly one role on this call?
"It will replace people"It replaces the manual work nobody wants to do. Your team moves to higher-value work. What would your team do with 10 hours back this week?
"We need to test first"Start with one agent, one workflow. If it misses the target by day 30, partial refund. What is the one task you would test?
"Our data is sensitive"Your data stays within your environment. Controlled scope, not blanket access. Do you have a data policy we should align with?
"We need HR approval"Good — HR protects the business. This is an external supplier system, not an internal hire. Can HR join the scoping call?
