The story

We didn't build another AI tool. We built the part that actually ships.

Human Architect spent years building AI agents for real businesses. The pattern was always the same. The model worked. The automation worked. Then it sat unused, because adopting it meant the business had to run the tech. Someone had to configure it, maintain it, fix it when it broke. So the business went back to doing the work by hand. We removed the business from the equation. NOHUMA is the result: we build the agent, host it on your own server, and run it every day. You get the outcome. That is the entire model. No setup, no maintenance, no hire. Just the work, done.

Non-Human Intelligence: your questions, answered.

What is a managed Non-Human Intelligence?

A managed Non-Human Intelligence is an autonomous agent built, onboarded, and fully operated inside your business for a flat monthly fee. Unlike a DIY tool, you never configure the tech. NOHUMA runs the agent end to end and delivers the outcome.

How fast is it live?

Your agent is live within 48 hours of the scoping call, or the first month is refunded. No setup fees, no discovery marathons.

What if it doesn't save us money?

If the agent has not saved you more than £5,000/month in human hours by day 30, the first month is refunded. No debate.

Is my data safe?

Your data stays within your environment. NOHUMA runs on an isolated client VPS with controlled, scoped access, not blanket permission.

How much does a Non-Human Intelligence cost in the UK?

NOHUMA is a flat £5,000 per month per client. Build, hosting, onboarding and operation included. No setup fees, no per-seat charges, no usage add-ons.

Can I hire a Non-Human Intelligence without a technical team?

Yes. NOHUMA is fully managed, so you need no developers, no IT setup, and no AI expertise. You bring one workflow to a 20-minute call; we build, host, and run the agent.

How is a managed Non-Human Intelligence different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT waits for prompts. A managed Non-Human Intelligence owns a workflow autonomously: chasing leads, triaging inboxes, sending reports on a schedule inside your Slack or Telegram.

Is NOHUMA a good AI agent for small businesses?

NOHUMA is built for UK SMEs turning over £1M–£2M. Each agent owns one recurring workflow: lead follow-up, inbox triage, ops reporting, so small teams get enterprise-grade automation without hiring technical staff.

Can I offer the agent to my own customers?

Yes. NOHUMA is an optional white-label. Offer the agent under your own brand, on your terms, single-tier with no recruitment. It becomes a revenue line, not a cost.
