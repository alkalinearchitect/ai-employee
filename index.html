04 / The call

What we'll ask in the 20 minutes.

No pitch deck. Three questions. If you can answer them, we can build it:

1

"What task costs you two hours every single day?"

If they name it instantly, we're already in. If they can't, they're not ready yet — and we'll say so.

2

"What happens when that task slips?"

This turns a time cost into a business risk. Risk justifies £5k a month faster than convenience ever will.

3

"What would you do with the hours back this week?"

Forces them to paint the outcome. If it's specific, we close. If it's vague, we wait.
