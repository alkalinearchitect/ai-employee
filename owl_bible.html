SECTION 01

The Category — why now

A managed AI employee is the 2026 equivalent of starting a Facebook-ads agency in 2013. The infrastructure just matured; the market has not caught up.

Until 2025, "AI for business" meant chatbots that answered questions and dashboards that needed a human to act. The managed-AI-employee model flips that: one agent, inside the client's workspace, owns a recurring outcome — not a conversation. The agent does the work, not just advises on it.

Proof the category is real (market evidence, not our product): Nick Vasilescu publicly reports closing £5k/month clients for design agencies, with his own Hermes agent building and onboarding the client agents. Greg Isenberg's May 2026 podcast productised the playbook: charge $5k/month for OpenClaw agents, $10k for Hermes agents, sell into legacy verticals. The model is no longer a bet — it is a pattern.
5clients @ £5k = £25k/mo = £300k ARR
2.5×less overhead than $2k pricing for same revenue
2026window is open now, not later

The opportunity is not "build AI." It is deploy and operate AI as a managed service for businesses that do not want to touch the stack. That is the seat Owl occupies.

SECTION 03

Brand DNA

Name — Owl

Owls see in the dark. They watch while you sleep. They act precisely. The name signals a quiet, always-on operator that catches what human teams miss at 1–2am and at scale. It is memorable, ownable, and marks cleanly.

Voice

  • Confident, not hyped. State outcomes. Never "revolutionise."
  • Operator-grade. Talk like the person who ships, not the vendor who pitches.
  • Plain English. No "synergy." No "leverage." No jargon a £1.5M-revenue owner can't repeat to their ops manager.
  • UK-specific. NI, pension, VAT, Ltd, "SMB" — not "SMBs" spelled the American way when it matters. Use £, not $.

Brand promise

"One agent. One outcome. Flat £5,000. Live in 48 hours — or the first month is refunded."

SECTION 04

Offer Architecture — the £5k engine

One price. Everything included. The simplicity is the product.

ComponentSpecification
Price£5,000 / month / client. No per-seat fees. No usage surprises.
ScopeOne agent, one defined outcome (lead follow-up, inbox triage, ops reporting, support).
SpeedLive within 48 hours of the scoping call.
GuaranteeIf it doesn't save >£5k/mo in human hours by day 30 → partial refund.
ChannelSlack or Telegram — where the client's team already lives.
IsolationOne profile, one bot token, one folder per client. One break never cascades.
UpsideClient may resell the build to their own customers (B2B2B).
Why £5k, not £2k: At $5k/mo, 5 clients = £300k ARR with 1 operator. At $2k, you need 12–13 clients for the same revenue — 2.5× the overhead for identical money. Price is a leverage decision, not a discount lever.

The OCA offer frame (from the proven playbook)

  1. Retainer up front. You are paid before you build.
  2. One full automation in <7 days. (Owl does 48h.)
  3. Money-back if not fulfilled. De-risks the yes.
  4. Then expand. Fleet → resell → second revenue line.

SECTION 07

Go-To-Market

Target verticals (legacy, cash-rich, automation-poor)

  • Marketing / creative agencies — lead follow-up, client reporting
  • Law firms — intake, conflict checks, doc triage
  • Insurance brokers — quote follow-up, claims inbox
  • Wholesale / distribution — order inbox, supplier comms
  • Real estate — viewing coordination, lead nurture
  • Manufacturing / trade — ops reporting, supplier chase

The X / LinkedIn playbook (from proven posting patterns)

  1. Lead with a result + a contrarian hook. "My agent closed another £5k client. I built none of it."
  2. Show the human's tiny role. "I ask the questions. The agent ships."
  3. Punch down on skepticism. Reply to "why pay £5k when ChatGPT is £20" with value: no maternity leave, no sick days, half the cost of a UK hire.
  4. Post same-day as wins. Multiple posts/day. Cadence > polish.
  5. Tag the ecosystem. Network effects compound reach.
