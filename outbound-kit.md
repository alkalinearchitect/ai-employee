# Outbound Kit — Demo Proof + First 10
_Use with the Dewey clone. One price. One outcome. £5k/mo._

## 1. Cold open — email
**Subject:** Your team spends X hours on Y every week
**Body (48 words max):**
You have a task that repeats every day. It’s manual, it’s expensive, and it never gets faster.
I run an AI employee that owns that exact task end to end. Flat £5k/month. Live inside Slack. You watch the result.
Do you want the 2-page scope for that one task?

## 2. LinkedIn DM — shorter
> Which task in your agency eats more than 10 hours a week?
> I run a managed AI employee that closes it. Flat £5k/mo.
> Want a 2-page scope for that exact task?

## 3. Target buckets
- Design agencies: intake, file QA, status updates
- Real estate: lead follow-up, booking confirmations
- Clinics/dental: appointment reminders, post-visit sequences
- Trades: quote chasing, review requests
- Professional services: doc checks, deadline reminders

Pick one bucket. Find 20 firms on Google Maps or LinkedIn. Send the DM. Move to email if no reply in 48h.

## 4. The one proof point to mention
“Design agency cut intake admin by 8+ hours/week. One Slack agent. Live in 48 hours.”
If they ask for evidence: send the fulfillment-layer page + 2-minute Loom link.

## 5. Booking mechanic
beacons.ai/humanarchitect → calendar → 20-minute scope call.
On the call: ask the three questions from the homepage. Then send one-page scope statement. Then invoice.

Track those numbers daily. Revenue follows the inputs.
